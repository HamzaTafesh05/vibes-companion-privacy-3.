Vibes Companion - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

1. About Vibes Companion

Vibes Companion is a personal-use WhatsApp bot currently being developed and tested with the WhatsApp Business Platform.

2. Information We Process

The bot may process WhatsApp phone numbers, incoming messages, message identifiers, and technical information required to operate the integration.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to receive messages, generate automated replies, troubleshoot technical issues, and test bot functionality.

4. Data Storage

Technical event records may be stored for duplicate-message prevention and reliability. The current WhatsApp integration does not create reminders from incoming messages.

5. Third-Party Services

The integration uses the WhatsApp Business Platform provided by Meta. Message processing is subject to applicable platform policies.
